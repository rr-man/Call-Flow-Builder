Flow canvas Drag a chip from the palette · drag a port dot onto a node to route · click a wire to edit it · Esc cancels

Drag a chip out of the palette and drop it where you want it. Move a node by its header. To route, drag a port dot onto a node — or click the dot once to arm it, then click the target. Valid targets stay lit; everything else dims. Click a wire to select it, then confirm the delete or cancel. ⏱ puts an Operation Times gate on any object. A .csv of DIDs or extensions can be dropped straight onto the canvas. V | H and + / − on an IVR set its orientation and whether unused keys are shown.
